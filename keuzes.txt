http://localhost:5000/overlay.html?veld=Mix2x
http://localhost:5000/live.html?veld=Mix2x

Beschikbare browsersource-varianten:
1. Eindscherm: http://localhost:5000/overlay.html?veld=Mix2x
2. Live timetrial: http://localhost:5000/live.html?veld=Mix2x

Extra velden kunnen worden gekozen door de querystring ?veld=... te wijzigen, bijvoorbeeld:
http://localhost:5000/overlay.html?veld=HC4+
http://localhost:5000/live.html?veld=HC4+
http://localhost:5000/overlay.html?veld=H2x
http://localhost:5000/live.html?veld=H2x
http://localhost:5000/overlay.html?veld=HC2+
http://localhost:5000/live.html?veld=HC2+
http://localhost:5000/overlay.html?veld=O1x
http://localhost:5000/live.html?veld=O1x
